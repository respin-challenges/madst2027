Baselines

Two baseline systems for each task and track: a cascade and an end-to-end model. Recipes and model weights will be released when the challenge opens.

Systems

Cascade
ASR, then machine translation with NLLB-200 or IndicTrans2, then TTS for speech-to-speech translation
End-to-end
A model fine-tuned from SeamlessM4T
TaskTrackBaselines
Speech-to-textConstrainedCascade (ASR, MT); end-to-end
Speech-to-textOpenCascade (ASR, MT); end-to-end
Speech-to-speechConstrainedCascade (ASR, MT, TTS); end-to-end
Speech-to-speechOpenCascade (ASR, MT, TTS); end-to-end
