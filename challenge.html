The challenge

How well can current models be adapted, with limited parallel data, to translate speech between any two of nine Indian languages, into text or directly into speech?

Motivation

Multilingual foundation models such as Whisper, SeamlessM4T and speech-capable large language models have made speech translation possible for many languages, but evaluation remains centred on English. Most Indian speech translation resources pair an Indian language with English, although translation between Indian languages is the more common need in India.

Bhojpuri, Chhattisgarhi, Magahi and Maithili have over 90 million speakers together (Census of India 2011).

The MADST Challenge builds on the RESPIN initiative and its two ASR challenges, MADASR at ASRU 2023 and MADASR 2.0 at ASRU 2025.

Languages and directions

Nine languages are included. Every ordered pair of different languages is a translation direction, giving 72 directions, and all of them are evaluated. Source and target language IDs are provided.

Tasks

Given an utterance and a target language, a system translates the utterance into that language. The two tasks have equal weight.

Task 1
Speech-to-text translation (S2TT). Output target-language text.
Task 2
Speech-to-speech translation (S2ST). Output target-language speech.

Teams can take part in one task or both.

Tracks

Each task has one leaderboard per track. The constrained track compares adaptation methods on equal resources; the open track measures what current resources can achieve.

TrackTraining dataPretrained models and external resources
ConstrainedChallenge training set onlyA fixed list of public checkpoints announced at launch, for example Whisper, MMS, SeamlessM4T, IndicTrans2 and NLLB-200

Expected outcomes

  • A public many-to-many benchmark with human reference text and speech in all 72 directions.
  • Evidence on cascade, end-to-end and speech-LLM approaches for languages with weak machine translation support.
  • Released data, baselines and evaluation scripts for research after the challenge.

Previous challenges

MADST follows two ASR challenges on RESPIN data:

  • MADASR, ASRU 2023
  • MADASR 2.0, ASRU 2025
