Data

Newly recorded read speech in nine languages, with human translations of every sentence and human reference speech for every development and test direction.

At a glance

Original sentences
About 20,500, between 2,000 and 3,000 per language, in standard dialects
Human translations
Over 140,000, from each original into the other eight languages
Speakers
270, 30 per language, balanced by gender
Utterances
81,000 newly recorded read-speech utterances, 300 sentences per speaker
Hours of speech
To be announced

Sentence groups

A group is one original sentence together with all of its translations. A group always stays in one split, so no sentence, in any language, appears in both training and test data. No speaker appears in more than one split.

Every development and test group is recorded in all nine languages. Each direction therefore has human reference text and human reference speech for every development and test sentence. Many training groups are also recorded in several languages, giving real speech-to-speech pairs.
