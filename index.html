Multilingual Any-Direction Speech Translation

Translate speech between any two of nine Indian languages, into text or directly into speech, with limited parallel data.

Two tasks, two tracks

Tasks and tracks in detail
Task 1

Speech-to-text translation

Given an utterance and a target language, output a translation as target-language text.

Task 2

Speech-to-speech translation

Given an utterance and a target language, output a translation as target-language speech.

Each task has one leaderboard per track. The two tasks have equal weight.
